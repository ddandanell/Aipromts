# Research Report: Black Hat SEO Growth Tactics

## 1. Summary
Analysis of aggressive SEO tactics used for rapid ranking manipulation, based on documented case studies and industry reports.

## 2. Evidence
- **Link schemes**: Buying or exchanging links at scale remains widespread despite Google penalties (Moz, 2023).
- **Cloaking**: Serving different content to search engines vs. users detected in 12% of penalized sites (Search Engine Journal, 2022).
- **Keyword stuffing**: Overuse of target keywords in hidden text still observed in low-quality niches.

## 3. Pattern Detection
- Black hat tactics cluster around link manipulation, content deception, and technical cloaking.
- Most tactics exploit temporary algorithmic blind spots before major Google core updates.
- High-risk/high-reward profiles: tactics can yield 3–6 months of elevated rankings before penalties.

## 4. Noise Filtering
- Excluded anecdotal forum posts without corroborating data.
- Removed tactics with no documented case studies post-2020.
